SLIDE 3
Problem
Problem statement
Verity  /  Final project defence
03
Weak identity checks can give an unverified agent access to customer onboarding.
Impersonation
A document, registration details and selfie may describe different people.
Presentation attacks
A face image alone cannot establish that the applicant is live.
Inconsistent decisions
Manual checks and isolated thresholds do not combine conflicting evidence consistently.
Duplicate or repeated attempts
Repeated identities and failed submissions can bypass an unstructured registration process.
Source: final report, §§1.2 and 2.3
